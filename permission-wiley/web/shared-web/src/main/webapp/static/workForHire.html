Work for Hire (WFH) - is a work created on behalf of Wiley where all parties agree in writing to the WFH designation. According to US copyright law, if a work is "made for hire", the company (Wiley)—not the source, vendor, artist, photographer or writer—is considered the legal owner and copyright holder, once the agreement/contract has been executed and the source has been paid. Future reference and credit of the asset will only be known as WILEY OWNED.
