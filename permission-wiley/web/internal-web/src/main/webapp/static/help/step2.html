STEP 2: FILTER YOUR SEARCH RESULTS

If your search yielded many results, you are able to filter your results in order to find the product you are looking for. There are many ways to do this. First, you can filter the results by manipulating the headings.

By clicking on a heading, you can organize the results in alphabetical order starting from A or Z, or in numerical order, from least to greatest.

You are also able to search within the results yielded. Just type within the search field that is located directly above the search result headings.

Quick Tip: You can also change how you would like to navigate your search results. If your search yields many results, navigate them by using the buttons in the bottom right-hand corner of the page.

Feel free to also change the number of results to appear on one page. The drop down menu is located on the top left-hand side of the page.
