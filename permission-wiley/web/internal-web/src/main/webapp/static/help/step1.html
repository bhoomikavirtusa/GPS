STEP 1: FIND YOUR PRODUCT

After logging in you will be lead to the homepage. From the homepage there are two ways to search for your desired product: basic search and advanced search. Both search fields are found in the upper right hand corner of the screen.

The basic search field searches by ISBN10 or ISBN13, Author Name, or Title.


If you click on advanced search you will be given more search criteria options. Advanced search allows you to search by Title, Author, Edition, Product Line, Editor Code, Copyright Year, Pub Status, and a combination of any and all of these criteria.

Note: This search method is mutually inclusive and only results that meet each criterion will be displayed.


To search, choose your desired search method, enter the product information, and click search products.

Quick Tip: Less is more when it comes to searching by title.
